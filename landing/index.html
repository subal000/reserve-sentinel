Why the rename

Same product. A sharper question.

ReserveSentinel started with one question: is the backing behind a tokenized stock really there?

Building it made a sharper question obvious. Backing matters — but what hurts people is the moment they try to leave. A token can show a price and a big liquidity number, and still hand you far less than that when you sell. For a lender, it's worse: a liquidation only works if someone can actually sell the collateral.

So the name now says what the product measures: whether a position can be unwound, and at what price.

What it measures

Three signals, one score, written on-chain.

Exit depth

What you'd actually receive selling your size, taken from live Jupiter quotes — not the liquidity figure an interface displays.

Price drift

How far the token trades from the real share, including over weekends when the stock market is shut and the token isn't.

Supply anomalies

Unusual minting and burning by the issuer — the kind of strain that shows up before any announcement does.

Who it's for

Lenders
